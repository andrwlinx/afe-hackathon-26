* Name: RampPath
* Description: RampPath is an evidence-backed knowledge graph that answers the question every new intern is afraid to ask: "who owns this, and who do I talk to?" You can ask in plain English or in a way specific to the package you're looking for, for example "Who owns AtlasRegionContext?", and RampPath traces verified ownership, access, and deployment paths across packages, pipelines, bindles, and AWS accounts, returning ranked people with explainable relevancy scores and a direct link to their Phone Tool profile. It's built for new AFE interns and any engineer joining an unfamiliar team, and it presents itself as a native tab inside Phone Tool.
